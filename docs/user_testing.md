# Real-User Test Record

**Project:** Football Laws Referee RAG  
**Live demo:** https://football-laws-rag-production.up.railway.app

The capstone usability test includes three users: Mohammed, Osamah, and Nabeel.

## Test protocol

Each tester uses the deployed application, reviews the answers and IFAB evidence, then gives short feedback on clarity, usefulness, usability, and overall experience.

---

## Tester 1 — Mohammed

- Answers clear: **Yes**
- Answers correct/useful: **Yes**
- Checked IFAB source/evidence: **No**
- What the tester liked: **The accuracy and precision of the answers.**
- Problem found: **None reported.**
- Overall rating (1–5): **5/5**

---

## Tester 2 — Osamah

- Answers clear: **Yes**
- Answers correct/useful: **Yes**
- Checked IFAB source/evidence: **No**
- What the tester liked: **The answers are short and clear.**
- Problem found: **There are only a few example questions.**
- Overall rating (1–5): **4.5/5**

---

## Tester 3 — Nabeel

- Answers clear: **Yes**
- Answers correct/useful: **Yes**
- Checked IFAB source/evidence: **Yes**
- What the tester liked: **The system is very smooth to use.**
- Problem found: **The UI/UX is not perfect and could be improved.**
- Overall rating (1–5): **4/5**

---

## Final usability summary

- Common strengths: All three testers found the answers clear and useful. Mohammed highlighted accuracy and precision, Osamah liked the short and clear answers, and Nabeel described the system as smooth to use.
- Common problems: Nabeel felt that the UI/UX could be improved, while Osamah noted that the interface provides only a few example questions. Mohammed reported no problem.
- Changes implemented: The feedback has been documented for the final capstone usability record. The most useful next interface improvements are expanding the example-question set and refining the UI/UX.
- Remaining limitations: Only one of the three testers explicitly checked the displayed IFAB evidence during the test, so future usability testing should encourage more users to inspect the supporting sources.
